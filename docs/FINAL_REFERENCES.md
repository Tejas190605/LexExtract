# LexExtract — Final Academic References & Bibliography

This bibliography compiles authentic academic papers, foundational textbooks, technical specifications, and official library documentation utilized throughout the design, implementation, and evaluation of LexExtract.

---

## 1. Academic Papers & Conference Proceedings

1. **Salton, G., & Buckley, C. (1988).** Term-weighting approaches in automatic text retrieval. *Information Processing & Management*, 24(5), 513–523.  
   *(Foundational paper on TF-IDF term weighting formulations in Information Retrieval).*

2. **Rose, S., Engel, D., Cramer, N., & Cowley, W. (2010).** Automatic Keyword Extraction from Individual Documents. In *Text Mining: Applications and Theory* (pp. 1–20). John Wiley & Sons, Ltd.  
   *(Introduced the RAKE algorithm using word co-occurrence graphs for unsupervised keyword extraction).*

3. **Mihalcea, R., & Tarau, P. (2004).** TextRank: Bringing Order into Texts. In *Proceedings of the 2004 Conference on Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing (EMNLP)* (pp. 404–411).  
   *(Graph-based ranking algorithm for keyword and sentence extraction).*

4. **Lafferty, J., McCallum, A., & Pereira, F. C. (2001).** Conditional Random Fields: Probabilistic Models for Segmenting and Labeling Sequence Data. In *Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Machine Learning (ICML)* (pp. 282–289).  
   *(Foundational statistical sequence tagging architecture for Named Entity Recognition).*

5. **Chalkidis, I., Fergadiotis, M., Malakasiotis, P., Aletras, N., & Androutsopoulos, I. (2020).** LEGAL-BERT: The Muppets straight out of Law School. In *Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EMNLP 2020* (pp. 2898–2904).  
   *(Investigated domain adaptation of transformer models for legal document analysis).*

6. **Hendrycks, D., Burns, C., Chen, A., Ball, S., & Song, D. (2021).** CUAD: An Expert-Annotated NLP Dataset for Legal Contract Review. In *Thirty-fifth Conference on Neural Information Processing Systems (NeurIPS 2021) Datasets and Benchmarks Track*.  
   *(Analysis of contract review benchmarks and legal NLP task definitions).*

7. **Zheng, L., Guha, N., Anderson, B. R., Henderson, P., & Ho, D. E. (2021).** When Does Pretraining Help? Assessing Self-Supervised Learning for Law and the CaseHOLD Dataset of 53,000+ Legal Holdings. In *Proceedings of the 18th International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Law (ICAIL)* (pp. 159–168).  
   *(Evaluated domain-specific linguistic models versus general models on judicial holdings).*

## 2. Textbooks & Reference Books

8. **Salton, G., & McGill, M. J. (1983).** *Introduction to Modern Information Retrieval*. McGraw-Hill, New York.  
   *(Classic textbook defining vector space models, precision, recall, and evaluation metrics).*

9. **Manning, C. D., Raghavan, P., & Schütze, H. (2008).** *Introduction to Information Retrieval*. Cambridge University Press.  
   *(Comprehensive textbook on indexing, vector space models, and ranked retrieval evaluation).*

10. **Jurafsky, D., & Martin, J. H. (2024).** *Speech and Language Processing: An Introduction to Natural Language Processing, Computational Linguistics, and Speech Recognition* (3rd ed. draft). Stanford University.  
    *(Covers n-gram language models, tokenization, stopword filtering, and sequence labeling).*

11. **Sommerville, I. (2015).** *Software Engineering* (10th ed.). Pearson.  
    *(Covers Three-Tier software architectures, requirements traceability, and testing methodologies).*

12. **Pressman, R. S., & Maxim, B. R. (2020).** *Software Engineering: A Practitioner's Approach* (9th ed.). McGraw-Hill Education.  
    *(Reference for software quality assurance, verification and validation, and risk management).*

15. **Fielding, R., Nottingham, M., & Reschke, J. (2022).** *HTTP Semantics*. RFC 9110, Internet Engineering Task Force (IETF).
