JUDGMENT
1. The present petition under Section 11(6) of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996 seeks appointment of a sole arbitrator to adjudicate disputes arising out of the Concession Agreement dated 12th March 2021.

2. The Petitioner contends that despite issuance of a formal notice invoking arbitration on 14th November 2025, the Respondent failed to concur upon the nomination of an independent arbitrator within thirty days as mandated under Section 11(4) of the Act.

3. The Respondent raises preliminary objections regarding territorial jurisdiction and pre-mature invocation, contending that amicable settlement under Clause 26 of the contract was not exhausted.

4. Having heard learned Senior Counsel for the parties, this Court observes that the scope of judicial inquiry under Section 11 is strictly confined to the examination of the prima facie existence of an arbitration agreement, as reaffirmed by the Supreme Court of India in Vidya Drolia v. Durga Trading Corporation.

5. In view of the admitted existence of the arbitration clause in the Concession Agreement, this petition is allowed. Hon'ble Mr. Justice R.V. Raveendran, former Judge of the Supreme Court of India, is appointed as the Sole Arbitrator. The arbitration proceedings shall be conducted under the aegis of the Delhi International Arbitration Centre (DIAC).
