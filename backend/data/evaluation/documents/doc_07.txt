TERM LOAN AGREEMENT AND EQUITABLE MORTGAGE

This Term Loan Agreement is executed on 18th March 2026 at Hyderabad, Telangana.

AMONGST:
State Bank of India, a statutory banking corporation constituted under the State Bank of India Act 1955, acting through its Stressed Assets Management Branch at Secunderabad (the "Lender");

AND:
Deccan Solar Power Projects Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act 2013 having its registered office at HITEC City, Hyderabad (the "Borrower");

AND:
K. V. Subba Rao, resident of Banjara Hills, Hyderabad (the "Personal Guarantor").

OPERATIVE TERMS:
1. SANCTION OF CREDIT FACILITY:
The Lender grants to the Borrower a rupee term loan facility up to a maximum principal sum of Rs. 15,00,00,000 (Rupees Fifteen Crores only) for setting up a 10 MW solar photovoltaic power generation project in Mehbubnagar.

2. INTEREST AND REPAYMENT SCHEDULE:
The term loan shall carry interest at SBI Marginal Cost of Funds Based Lending Rate (MCLR) plus a spread of 2.75% per annum, with monthly rests. The principal sum shall be repayable in 24 equal quarterly installments commencing 1st April 2027.

3. SECURITY AND MORTGAGE:
The Borrower creates an equitable mortgage by deposit of title deeds under Section 58(f) of the Transfer of Property Act 1882 over 45 acres of freehold agricultural and industrial conversion land situated at Survey No. 112, Mehbubnagar.

4. EVENTS OF DEFAULT:
In the event of failure to pay interest or principal on the due date, the Lender shall be entitled to initiate recovery proceedings under Section 13(2) of the SARFAESI Act 2002 and invoke insolvency resolution under Section 7 of the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code 2016 before the National Company Law Tribunal (NCLT).
