MINISTRY OF LAW AND JUSTICE
(LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT)
New Delhi, the 1st January 2026

THE COMPANIES (SUSTAINABILITY AND DIGITAL DISCLOSURE) AMENDMENT ORDINANCE 2026
No. 1 of 2026

Promulgated by the President in the Seventy-seventh Year of the Republic of India.

An Ordinance further to amend the Companies Act 2013.

WHEREAS Parliament is not in session and the President is satisfied that circumstances exist which render it necessary for her to take immediate action;

NOW, THEREFORE, in exercise of the powers conferred by clause (1) of Article 123 of the Constitution of India, the President is pleased to promulgate the following Ordinance:

1. SHORT TITLE AND COMMENCEMENT:
(1) This Ordinance may be called the Companies (Sustainability and Digital Disclosure) Amendment Ordinance 2026.
(2) It shall come into force at once.

2. AMENDMENT OF SECTION 134:
In Section 134 of the Companies Act 2013, after sub-section (3), the following clause shall be inserted:
"(ca) a certified statement of carbon emissions and digital governance resilience in such form as may be prescribed by the Central Government."

3. INSERTION OF NEW SECTION 454B (PENALTY FOR REPEATED DEFAULT):
Whoever fails to comply with corporate disclosure norms shall be liable to a penalty of Rs. 5,00,000, and in case of continuing failure, with a further penalty of Rs. 50,000 for each day during which the default continues, subject to a maximum of Rs. 1,00,00,000. Any appeal against an adjudication order passed by the Registrar of Companies shall lie before the Regional Director under Section 454(5).
