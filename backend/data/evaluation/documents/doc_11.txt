GENERAL POWER OF ATTORNEY

TO ALL TO WHOM THESE PRESENTS SHALL COME, I, Brig. Harish Chandra Varma (Retd.), son of Late Shri J. P. Varma, residing at Defence Colony, New Delhi (the "Principal"), SEND GREETINGS:

WHEREAS I am the sole and absolute registered owner of residential plot bearing No. B-14, Green Park Extension, New Delhi;

AND WHEREAS owing to my advanced age and frequent overseas medical travel, I am unable to personally manage, supervise, and execute necessary legal deeds in respect of the said property.

NOW KNOW YE ALL AND THESE PRESENTS WITNESS that I hereby nominate, constitute, and appoint my daughter, Meera Varma Sen, resident of Vasant Kunj, New Delhi (the "Attorney"), as my true and lawful attorney to act for me and on my behalf:

1. MANAGEMENT AND LICENSING:
To lease, let, license, or grant tenancy in respect of the property, collect rents, issue receipts, and discharge municipal taxes payable to the Municipal Corporation of Delhi.

2. LITIGATION AND LEGAL PROCEEDINGS:
To institute, defend, prosecute, compromise, or settle any legal suit, civil petition, or eviction proceeding before the High Court of Delhi, District Courts, or Rent Controller under the Delhi Rent Control Act 1958.

3. CONVEYANCE AND SALE:
To negotiate terms, execute agreements to sell, and appear before the Sub-Registrar of Assurances at New Delhi to register conveyances under the Registration Act 1908.

4. RATIFICATION:
I hereby ratify and confirm all lawful acts, deeds, and matters done by my said Attorney by virtue of this Power of Attorney.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, I have executed this deed on this 12th day of February 2026 at New Delhi.
