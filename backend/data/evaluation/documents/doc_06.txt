SUBJECT: Notice of Dispute and Invocation of Arbitration under Clause 19 of EPC Contract No. EPC/2022/88.

Sir/Madam,
Under instructions from our Client, Vertex Heavy Engineering Ltd, we state as follows:

1. Under the Engineering, Procurement, and Construction Agreement dated 14th June 2022, our Client was engaged as the Sub-Contractor for mechanical fabrication and structural erection works.

2. In terms of Milestone Certificate No. 4, an aggregate sum of Rs. 8,45,00,000 became due and payable to our Client as on 30th September 2025. Despite submission of progressive invoices and joint measurement sheets, you have deliberately withheld payment in breach of contract.

3. Clause 19.2 of the Agreement stipulates that in the event of any dispute arising out of or in connection with the contract, the dispute shall be resolved through arbitration by a panel of three arbitrators in New Delhi under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996.

4. PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that in terms of Section 21 of the Act, arbitral proceedings in respect of the dispute stand commenced upon receipt of this notice. Our Client hereby nominates Hon'ble Mr. Justice Badar Durrez Ahmed, former Chief Justice of the High Court of Jammu & Kashmir, as its nominee arbitrator.

5. You are hereby called upon to nominate your arbitrator within 30 days of receipt hereof, failing which our Client shall approach the High Court of Delhi under Section 11(6) of the Act.
