M/s QuickBite Foods LLP,

Advocates for: BurgerBite Global Brands Inc., Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

SUBJECT: Cease and Desist Notice for Infringement of Registered Trademark 'BURGERBITE' and Passing Off.

Sir/Madam,
We act for BurgerBite Global Brands Inc. (hereinafter our "Client").

1. Our Client is the registered proprietor of the well-known trademark 'BURGERBITE' registered under Class 43 of the Trade Marks Act 1999 under Registration No. 3419082 dated 14th August 2012. Our Client has established substantial goodwill and reputation across India.

2. It has recently come to our Client's attention that you are operating retail quick-service restaurants under the deceptively similar mark 'QUICK BITE BURGER' and utilizing a colour scheme identical to our Client's registered trade dress.

3. Your deliberate adoption of an identical phonetic and visual mark constitutes statutory trademark infringement under Section 29 of the Trade Marks Act 1999 and the common law tort of passing off, intending to deceive consumers.

4. WE HEREBY CALL UPON YOU to immediately:
(a) Cease and desist from using the mark 'QUICK BITE BURGER' or any deceptively similar mark;
(b) Deliver up for destruction all advertising materials, hoardings, signage, and packaging;
(c) Pay Rs. 15,00,000 towards damages for dilution of goodwill.

Failure to comply within 15 days will compel our Client to institute an infringement suit before the High Court of Judicature at Madras seeking permanent injunction and rendition of accounts under Section 134 and Section 135 of the Trade Marks Act 1999.
