DEED OF DISSOLUTION OF PARTNERSHIP

This Deed of Dissolution is made on this 28th day of February 2026 at Ahmedabad, Gujarat.

BETWEEN:
1. Rajeshbhai Patel, residing at Satellite, Ahmedabad (hereinafter referred to as the "Continuing Partner");
AND:
2. Mukeshbhai Shah, residing at Navrangpura, Ahmedabad (hereinafter referred to as the "Retiring Partner").

WHEREAS the parties hereto have been carrying on business in partnership under the registered firm name 'Patel & Shah Logistics' under a Partnership Deed dated 1st April 2019 registered under the Indian Partnership Act 1932.

AND WHEREAS mutual differences have arisen and the partners have mutually agreed to dissolve the partnership firm with effect from 28th February 2026 on the following terms:

1. DISSOLUTION AND WINDING UP:
The partnership firm stands dissolved with effect from close of business on 28th February 2026 pursuant to Section 40 of the Indian Partnership Act 1932.

2. SETTLEMENT OF CAPITAL ACCOUNTS:
The Continuing Partner has paid to the Retiring Partner a sum of Rs. 65,00,000 (Rupees Sixty Five Lakhs only) by RTGS transfer towards full and final settlement of capital balance, goodwill, and share in accrued profits.

3. LIABILITIES AND INDEMNITY:
The Continuing Partner takes over all assets, stock-in-trade, and commercial liabilities of the firm. The Continuing Partner indemnifies the Retiring Partner against all tax demands, vendor claims, and statutory dues under the Central Goods and Services Tax Act 2017.

4. PUBLIC NOTICE:
Public notice of dissolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and local newspapers in accordance with Section 72 of the Indian Partnership Act 1932.
