COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is executed on this 1st day of October 2026 at Gurugram, Haryana.

BETWEEN:
DLF Cyber City Developers Ltd, a company incorporated under the Companies Act 2013 having its registered office at Cyber City, Gurugram, Haryana (hereinafter referred to as the "Lessor", which expression shall include its successors and assigns) of the FIRST PART;

AND:
Nexus Global Technologies Pvt Ltd, a private limited company having its principal office at Sector 44, Gurugram (hereinafter referred to as the "Lessee", which expression shall include its successors and permitted assigns) of the SECOND PART.

WHEREAS the Lessor is the absolute owner of the commercial demised premises situated at Unit 402, Tower B, Cyber Green, Gurugram comprising an area of 5,000 square feet.

NOW THEREFORE IT IS MUTUALLY AGREED AS FOLLOWS:
1. LEASE TERM AND RENT:
The Lessor hereby demises unto the Lessee the premises for a term of 36 months commencing from 1st October 2026. The Lessee shall pay a monthly rent of Rs. 4,50,000 (Rupees Four Lakh Fifty Thousand only) payable in advance by the 7th day of each calendar month.

2. SECURITY DEPOSIT:
The Lessee has deposited an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 27,00,000 with the Lessor, refundable upon termination of this lease.

3. COVENANTS AND MAINTENANCE:
The Lessee shall pay applicable electricity charges and maintenance charges. The Lessee covenants not to sub-let or assign the demised premises without prior written consent.

4. INDEMNITY:
The Lessee agrees to indemnify and hold harmless the Lessor against all liabilities, damages, and claims arising from breach of any covenant.

5. TERMINATION AND JURISDICTION:
Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving three months prior written notice pursuant to Section 106 of the Transfer of Property Act 1882. Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration in Gurugram under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act 1996. The courts at Gurugram shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
